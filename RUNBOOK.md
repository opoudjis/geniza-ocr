# RUNBOOK: now in `runbooks/`

From release v1.4 (27 October 2026) the project's runbooks are published in the folder [`runbooks/`](runbooks/). Each has an undated file, which is always a copy of its newest dated version, and the dated versions published before it, kept unchanged.

- **The cupel run sheet**, how a batch of scans is brought into the OCR corpus and given its absence class, steps C0 to C13: [`runbooks/CUPEL-RUN-SHEET.md`](runbooks/CUPEL-RUN-SHEET.md). This is what `RUNBOOK.md` held in v1.3. References to `RUNBOOK.md` in `METHOD.md` and `docs/` mean this file.
- **The dowse runbook**, how the facts about people are read out of a source, source by source: [`runbooks/DOWSE-RUNBOOK.md`](runbooks/DOWSE-RUNBOOK.md).
- **The smelt runbook**, how one person's facts are grouped, checked and written up as a biography (a draft): [`runbooks/SMELT-RUNBOOK.md`](runbooks/SMELT-RUNBOOK.md).

The earlier versions of the cupel procedure:

- [`runbooks/CUPEL-RUN-SHEET-2026-10-22.md`](runbooks/CUPEL-RUN-SHEET-2026-10-22.md): the run sheet as published in v1.3.
- [`runbooks/CUPEL-RUNBOOK-2026-10-03.md`](runbooks/CUPEL-RUNBOOK-2026-10-03.md): the runbook as published in v1.0 to v1.2.
