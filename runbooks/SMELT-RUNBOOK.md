## What smelt is

The dowse digs out the raw facts, source by source, and the harvest rolls them up into one profile per person. **Smelt works on one person's harvested facts** and separates the metal from the slag: it groups the lines that tell the same fact, checks them, builds the episodes the narrative needs, and writes the biography: the sense-making, QA and making accessible (2026-10-13).

- **Input:** the person's profile (`profiles/<stem>.md`) and their rows in `profiles/build/FACTS-LIST.tsv` (with the event kinds), the names register, the event calendar.
- **Output:** a fact-groups report, its check, the episodes and groups, the biography, and proposals for the names register.
- **The interface with the dowse** is the facts line and its template (`DOWSE-TEMPLATE-FACTS-WRITING-EO-2026-10-10.md`), owned by the dowse. Smelt never edits a facts file: a fault it finds goes back to the dowse (S5).
- Trigger: «smelt <person>».

## S1 · Fact groups, by tool

`tools/fact-groups.py` («The commands»), the entity match, the default since 2026-10-14 (`SMELT-GROUPING-PROPOSAL-2026-10-14.md`, with the three rounds of the reader check):
- Two lines are **one fact** when they have the same person, the same kind, the same object and compatible dates. **Named-thing kinds** (meetings, quarrels, works, joinings, places …) match on the object's head with canonical names («POSTNIKOV» = «Postnikov (Fjodor)»); other kinds on the whole object (hyphens and punctuation folded, case kept, years dropped).
- **Dates are compared pairwise,** never chained (`date_vocab.compatible`: the ranges overlap, «about» widened by one unit). A less precise date or a bare name joins a more precise one only when there is exactly one candidate in the corpus («1907» joins «1907-03», but not when «1907-05» is there too). An undated line joins a group only where its kind and object have one dated group.
- A line also joins the groups of a **second kind** that carries an object (`+ofico: membro, Lingva Komitato, eksigita`).
- **The relational kinds** (renkonto, konflikto, interkonsento, influo) merge only on the same exact day or the same whole object; their near-matches are listed as **candidates** for the episode reader, not merged.
- **Nominations:** a line whose whole object is a group's, with another date, is nominated as a possible date error.
- **Kept apart:** works keep their numbers; lines on the differences between copies; refuted claims; «alia» and «samnomo» are never grouped; lines without a kind are counted and left out.
- **Groups are atomic:** «president in 1912» and «president in 1913» are two facts; the span of office is built at the episode level (S3).
- **Open (2026-10-15):** the pairwise date rule keeps 1912-12-13 and 1913 apart, while the episode reader is told they «proksimumas»; a tolerance at the year boundary is accepted for probing, with the editor's caution that it is fuzzy matching (`GROUP-IDS-PROPOSAL-2026-10-15.md`, «Five design rules», 2). **Probed 2026-10-16 22:3x** (the editor to three terms: a bare year against a day or month only, within four weeks outside it, never two precise dates; a candidate for the episode reader, never merged; every pair listed in full): `tools/fact-groups.py --year-edge DAYS` (off by default; the pairs go to a list no other tool reads). ## S2 · Checking the groups

`smelt-s2-prep.py ‹tag› ‹stem›=‹NAME› [--kinds ‹kinds›]` («The commands») writes each person's check input and brief (`smelt/<stem>/S2-CHECK-INPUT-<tag>.md`, `S2-CHECK-BRIEF-<tag>.md`, from `SMELT-S2-CHECK-BRIEF-TEMPLATE-EO-2026-10-14.md`). A file-reader per brief; the return is filed as `S2-CHECK-RETURN-<tag>.md`. A sample, read against the frozen profile (the first check: `profiles/samples/de-beaufront-grupoj-kontrolo-2026-10-13.md`):
- about a dozen groups, weighted to the risky ones (the largest, those with differences, lines in two groups);
- for each, whether every line tells **the same fact** (not the same moment, not the same theme), and whether the differences are stated; for a few, a search of the profile for lines the group missed;
- the faults to look for first: a line grouped by its moment or theme, not its predicate (the 1877 typhus under «became a tutor»); a line that mentions a date in passing pulled into that date's group; two lines from one page counted as two witnesses; an object too coarse; an object without the body's main form;
- the result in `smelt/<stem>/FACT-GROUPS-CHECK-<date>.md`; faults in the lines go back to the dowse (S5).

## S3 · Episodes, by a reader

An episode is an event with its circumstances (the joining with where he lived, who brought him the advertisement, his later retelling), or a span (an office held 1904–1908), built on the fact groups. **The brief:** `ocr/method/SMELT-S3-EPISODE-BRIEF-TEMPLATE-EO-2026-10-14.md`, filled by `smelt-s2-prep.py --episodes` («The commands»), which writes `smelt/<stem>/S3-INPUT-<tag>.md` (every group, candidate and nomination, each line by its L) and `S3-BRIEF-<tag>.md`. The reader also groups the lines without a kind. The return is filed as `S3-RETURN-<tag>.md`. The test case is De Beaufront (2026-10-14).

The reader's rules (the template's numbering):
- membership by the line's own predicate, never by its date or theme (1); two lines from one page are one witness; a copy is no new witness (3);
- **«malsamas»** for a difference of fact; **«proksimumas»** where values differ only in precision, sit either side of a boundary, or are rounded; two stages of one thing (a draft and its printing) are no disagreement; **«ŝajnfakto»** for a late, unsourced retelling that a primary contradicts (4);
- **one group per fact, its judgements in it:** a line that judges or explains the group's fact goes under «juĝoj» (author, kind, date), never under «malsamas» or into the statement, and is no witness; a judgement about the person or an episode is a group of its own; a judgement line without its own kind or date is a dowse fault (4a);
- **a group's statement is a full stand-alone fact,** by the facts-writing rules; every group listed, single lines too (5);
- groups atomic, spans of office built in the episode (6);
- **one line per episode and per group,** breaks written `\n`; the sign-off «Fino: N epizodoj, M grupoj.»

**The reading copy:** `tools/smelt-view.py ‹stem› ‹tag›` («The commands») writes `smelt/<stem>/S3-VIEW-<tag>.md`: every episode, group and candidate followed by its lines in full, each with its source and locator, for adjudication and reading. The return is the record.

## S4 · The biography, by a reader

**The brief:** `ocr/method/BIOGRAPHY-BRIEF-TEMPLATE-EO-2026-10-14.md`, filled by `smelt-s2-prep.py --biography` («The commands») on the tag's frozen profile and S3 return, as `smelt/<stem>/S4-BRIEF-<tag>.md`. (The 2026-10-12 template is kept only as the record of the «before» biography.) Its rules:
- **sources:** the episodes and groups, the frozen profile (for checking and citing), the names register; nothing else;
- **the form:** a summary of about 100 words, **without citations**; the biography, one section per period of the life, written short («a tool for finding the sources, not a book»); in **a long biography** (more than four periods, or about 2,000 words) each period opens with two or three sentences, without citations and with nothing not in its section, and the summary is written from them; **open conflicts** at the end (of opinion or interpretation only); **notes on minor discrepancies** (one deviant witness against several is an error, noted, not promoted);
- **paragraphs of at most 8 sentences**; **every sentence outside the summaries cites its lines** («[L12, L45]»);
- the groups and episodes are the structure; a departure from them is said in the report;
- an uncontested fact plainly, with all its lines; a contested fact as the best evidence shows it, crediting the one who proved it by their own research, naming briefly who contests it, not listing those who repeat; a claim made before the proof is worth a mention, a repetition after it is not;
- opinion, conjecture, judgement always with the name of who says it (in a collective volume, the contribution's author); the person's own account is a contestant; anecdotes and characterisations belong, attributed;
- dates: a context guess («divene») is never written as a date; an assertion is dated by its saying;
- **the report:** the summary, biography, conflicts and notes; the episodes not used, and why; where it departed from the groups or episodes; where the profile looked unclear, and register proposals; the sign-off «Fino.».
